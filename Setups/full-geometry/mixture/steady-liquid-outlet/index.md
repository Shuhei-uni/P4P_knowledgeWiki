## Scientific role

The campaign established the direction for the next step: increasingly restrictive outlet conditions could retain more Y010/Y030 liquid before the steady solver failed, and selected mass-flow cases showed distinct behavior worth revisiting transiently.

This is **screening evidence**, not a claim that a particular steady outlet formulation is physically correct.
