1 · Objective

Determine whether the Skoog-style allocated DPM population deposits into a bounded Eulerian wall film and drains without enabling splash, edge separation, or particle stripping. This is the clean control for all later 010V2 interaction branches.

The first execution is an EWF stability baseline: EWF–DPM coupling is enabled so injected droplets can deposit into the film, while global DPM Interaction with Continuous Phase is disabled. The resulting film behavior is the uncoupled global-DPM control; 010V2d-2 is reserved for the corresponding combined case with global DPM interaction enabled.

Interpretation target Observe deposition, storage, and drainage in isolation before attributing behavior to splash, edge separation, stripping, or two-way global DPM feedback.

2 · Inherited setup

Start from a saved, read-back-verified 09cV2 case/data pair. Do not start from the existing 10a artifact because it is splash-enabled and uses the old branch identity.

Keep unchanged

  • Geometry, mesh, split inlet topology, outlet boundaries, gravity, and operating pressure.
  • Mixture, phase definitions, carrier materials, RNG k-epsilon, and Energy off.
  • The 09cV2 liquid/DPM allocation and EWF-compatible DPM material names.
  • Injection diameters, flows, velocities, particle count, drag, rotation, and stochastic settings.
  • DPM outlet and ordinary-wall fates unless the wall must change to wall-film for coupling.
Do not inherit The failed 10a data field, its water-liquid DPM material, or its splash/stripping state.

Intentional live-case controls and the single DPM correction

The server-4 readback confirmed that the other deviations from the provisional plan are intentional for this execution. Preserve the configured EWF options, fixed transient controls, water-liquid-at-psep / water-liquid-at-psep-dpm material identities and properties, global-DPM state, and reflect wall fate as currently set.

Only DPM tracking corrections required before execution
  • Disable Unsteady Particle Tracking.
  • Remove the 0.001 s particle-time-step override and return to inherited parent tracking.
  • Restore Maximum Number of Steps from 500 to 10000.

These three corrections are inherited by 010V2a–010V2d.

4 · Click-by-click build procedure

A · Save the 09cV2 parent
  1. Open the read-back-verified 09cV2 case/data pair.
  2. Go to File > Save Case/Data As.
  3. Save as 010V2-ewf-deposition-control.cas.h5 and 010V2-ewf-deposition-control.dat.h5.
  4. Preserve the 09cV2 case/data pair separately.
B · Enable and configure EWF
  1. Go to Models > Eulerian Wall Film > Edit.
  2. Enable Eulerian Wall Film.
  3. Set Solve Momentum = On and select Momentum Equation.
  4. Enable Gravity Force and Surface Shear Force.
  5. Enable Pressure Gradient if exposed; record whether it was available.
  6. Leave Spreading Term, Surface Tension, Solve Energy, and Solve Scalar off.
  7. Select the verified project film material and enable DPM Coupling.
  8. Leave Phase Coupling, VOF Coupling, and Treat Sharp Edge off.
  9. Confirm Particle Splashing, Edge Separation, and Particle Stripping are off.
  10. Click Apply/OK.
C · Assign the EWF wall
  1. Go to Boundary Conditions.
  2. Select the confirmed physical liquid-impact wall zone, initially wall only.
  3. Open the Wall Film tab and select the Eulerian wall-film condition.
  4. Set initial film height to 0 m.
  5. Set initial film velocity to 0 m/s in every component.
  6. Set Flow Momentum Coupling = Off.
  7. Set DPM wall interaction to the standard wall-film/impingement path, not trap or ordinary reflect.
  8. Leave wall splash off.
  9. Confirm bottom is not accidentally assigned as an EWF wall unless intentionally part of the film path.
  10. Inspect every film-wall edge; confirm it connects to another film wall or an intentional drain/outflow edge.
  11. Click Apply, then reopen the wall panel and verify the readback.
D · Check material and injection compatibility
  1. In Models > Eulerian Wall Film, record the exact film-material name.
  2. Go to Models > Discrete Phase > Injections.
  3. Confirm every EWF-coupled injection material has the same relevant properties as the film material.
  4. Confirm injection names contain the film-material name.
  5. Confirm all six flows sum to the selected 09cV2 DPM total. Do not assume 5.846 kg/s unless the selected fraction is explicitly 5%.
  6. If a material/name check fails, stop before initialization, correct the material identity, and record the change.
E · Set transient film solution controls
  1. In General, change Time from Steady to Transient.
  2. In Solution Methods, set transient time discretization to First Order Implicit.
  3. Keep carrier equations at the accepted parent schemes.
  4. In EWF Solution Method and Control, set wall-film continuity and momentum schemes to the most conservative first-order option exposed.
  5. Keep Coupled Solution off.
  6. Retain fixed control: 1.0e-5 s, 40 steps, and 1 iteration per step.
  7. Do not enable adaptive time stepping unless it is a separate sensitivity.
  8. If exposed, set Max Courant Number = 0.5 as the Assumed conservative starting value. Keep film-step increase/decrease factors at Fluent defaults, confirm both exceed 1, and record readbacks.
  9. Retain Per Flow Iterations = 1, Reporting Interval = 1, Sub-Iteration Stop = 1e-8, Sub-Iterations = 10, and sub-iteration Reporting Interval = 1, unless a separate numerical sensitivity is documented.
  10. In DPM Control, set Relaxation Factor = 0.5 as the Assumed conservative starting value and retain DPM per Film Steps = 20.
  11. The film-specific relaxation factor belongs in EWF Solution Method and Control, not general Solution Controls.
  12. Keep global DPM Interaction with Continuous Phase = Off through 010V2d; enable it only in 010V2d-2.
  13. Do not copy Skoog's BWR time step as a project default. Preserve the checkpoint before changing the fixed timestep after a film-CFL or source spike.
F · Initialize the film and run
  1. Save the case before initialization.
  2. Go to Solution > Initialization.
  3. Initialize the carrier field with inherited parent data if valid; otherwise use Hybrid Initialization and record the fallback.
  4. In Models > Eulerian Wall Film, click Initialize for wall-film variables.
  5. Confirm film height and velocity are zero on the selected wall.
  6. Run 5–10 transient time steps as the first smoke test.
  7. After every step, monitor continuity, phase fraction, k, epsilon, film Courant number, film mass, film source terms, and DPM-to-film mass transfer.
  8. If film CFL or source terms spike, stop and preserve the checkpoint before changing the fixed timestep. Do not interpret a floating-point exception as a mechanism result.
  9. If stable, continue to 20–50 transient steps, then a documented averaging window after film inventory develops.
  10. Save case/data checkpoints without overwriting 09cV2.

5 · Required monitors and reports

Film state

  • Area-weighted film thickness on each EWF wall
  • Film mass/inventory versus time
  • Film velocity and drainage direction
  • Maximum film thickness
  • Film CFL history

Film mass pathways

  • Film DPM Mass Source
  • Film Outflow Mass
  • Film Stripped Mass = zero
  • Film Separated Mass = zero

Carrier and DPM evidence

  • Steam-outlet liquid phase flux
  • Actual global DPM and EWF–DPM coupling states
  • DPM escaped, trapped, and incomplete counts
  • Residual history and complete phase-flux imbalance
