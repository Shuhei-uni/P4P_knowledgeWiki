# Wiki change discipline

## Before writing

- State key assumptions if the request has more than one reasonable reading.
- When routing is ambiguous, identify the likely owner and why before editing.
- If source evidence is unclear, label the uncertainty; do not silently fill gaps.

## While editing

- Add only the content required to answer the request or preserve its evidence trail.
- Prefer linked summaries over duplication.
- Touch only the primary page, required cross-references, and the index or log updates required by local contracts.
- Match the target page's existing structure, citations, and uncertainty labels.
- Do not rewrite adjacent material merely for polish.
- Do not remove or relabel prior assumptions, blockers, or failed experiments unless evidence supersedes them.
- Mention unrelated stale or duplicate content rather than correcting it opportunistically.

## Completion check

For multi-step work, verify that the requested content is in the correct owner, citations and uncertainty information are preserved or added, and all required cross-links, indexes, logs, and setup-order references are aligned. Then check the changed files against their local `AGENTS.md` contracts.
