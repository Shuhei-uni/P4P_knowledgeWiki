# Phase 3 — DPM carryover and coupling

## Scientific purpose

Introduce and qualify dispersed-droplet carryover, stochastic dispersion,
two-way coupling, and fine-mist loading on the parity carrier.

## Retained experiments

- [purnanto-09a-dpm-deterministic-carryover](purnanto-09a-dpm-deterministic-carryover/results.md) — reported deterministic diagnostic with incomplete tracks.
- [purnanto-09b-dpm-stochastic-dispersion](purnanto-09b-dpm-stochastic-dispersion/results.md) — reported stochastic diagnostic; not an efficiency curve.
- [purnanto-09c-two-way-dpm-coupling](purnanto-09c-two-way-dpm-coupling/results.md) — partial carrier-only coupled diagnostic.
- [purnanto-09cV2-dpm-partition-control](purnanto-09cV2-dpm-partition-control/results.md) — executed DPM-fate diagnostic with non-converged carrier.
- [purnanto-09cV3-fine-mist-psd](purnanto-09cV3-fine-mist-psd/results.md) — mixed completed, partial, and interrupted fine-mist diagnostics.

## Phase outcome

The retained runs show that DPM fate and carryover are sensitive to particle
size, stochastic dispersion, and loading/coupling choices, while incomplete
tracks and open carrier balance prevent a validated or report-facing
carryover claim.

## Remaining uncertainty

The fine-mist branches include non-canonical checkpoints and incomplete
continuations. Their evidence cannot be combined with the later EWF family
without a matched, sufficiently settled carrier and explicit mass closure.
