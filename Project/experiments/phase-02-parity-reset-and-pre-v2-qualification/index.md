# Phase 2 — Parity reset and pre-V2 qualification

## Scientific purpose

Establish the 08b parity lineage and investigate carrier/inlet behaviour,
including pre-V2 full-geometry pressure and model-form sensitivity, before the
Full Geometry V2 production campaign.

## Retained experiments

- [purnanto-08b-parity-split-inlet](purnanto-08b-parity-split-inlet/results.md) — executed, non-converged parity diagnostic.
- [purnanto-08c-inlet-loading-sensitivity](purnanto-08c-inlet-loading-sensitivity/results.md) — executed partial/non-converged sensitivity diagnostic.
- [purnantov2-08-one-inlet-massflow-recreation](purnantov2-08-one-inlet-massflow-recreation/technical-reconstruction-smoke-test.md) — retained conservatively as an executed 10- and 500-iteration smoke/diagnostic reconstruction; not Phase 5 production lineage.
- [full-geometry-02c-mixture-pressure-sensitivity](full-geometry-02c-mixture-pressure-sensitivity/results.md) — executed non-converged/unstable pressure screen.
- [full-geometry-vof-02d-model-form-sensitivity](full-geometry-vof-02d-model-form-sensitivity/setup.md) — executed but inconclusive transient VOF diagnostic; attempts stopped at 67–69 steps.

## Phase outcome

The 08b/08c, 02c, and 02d records provide executed parity, loading, pressure,
and model-form response evidence, but open phase balance, early numerical
failures, and incomplete transient qualification prevent promotion of a
qualified carrier baseline or a selected operating pressure/model.

## Remaining uncertainty

The purnantov2-08 smoke record is retained because its historical notes report
10- and 500-iteration Fluent diagnostics, but its relationship to the later
parity lineage is limited and should not be treated as a production case.
