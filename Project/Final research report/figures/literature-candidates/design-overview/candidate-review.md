# Candidate figures from Zarrouk and Purnanto (2015)

Source: *Geothermal steam-water separators: Design overview*, Geothermics 53, 236–254, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geothermics.2014.05.009). Reviewed against Sections 2.1–2.3 of the current report. The local PDF is [Zarrouk and Purnanto 2014.pdf](<../../../../../CFD_wiki/raw/Zarrouk and Purnanto 2014.pdf>). The report's bibliography year remains 2015; the local PDF running header uses 2014.

All pages containing figures were inspected visually. Previews are unaltered full pages, including the highlights present in the local PDF. Shortlisted pages are rendered at 200 dpi; other surveyed pages are 120 dpi. These are review artifacts rather than report-ready crops.

Suggested minimum: Figures 10, 12 and 14. Figure 13 is useful beside Figure 14 but may be redundant. Figure 5 is context rather than direct support for the study's selected numerical investigations.

## Attribution and reuse record

The paper states “© 2014 Elsevier Ltd. All rights reserved.” This inspection did not establish a figure-reuse licence. The original captions' secondary credits are recorded above; they should be carried into attribution if the corresponding images are reproduced. A genuinely original conceptual diagram should cite the supporting sources and should not be labelled as a reproduction of published CFD results. No permission request or external contact was made.
