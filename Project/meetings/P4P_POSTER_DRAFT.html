The design question

How can CFD guide lower liquid carryover? Building on Purnanto et al. [1], we assess outlet predictions alongside conservation and inventory.

Model and comparison basis

  • Carrier: steady Mixture model; RNG k–ε turbulence.
  • Geometry: common simplified mesh, 60,964 cells; closed bottom.
  • Reference feed: liquid 116.94 kg/s; vapour 80.70 kg/s.
  • Speed range: 20.11–32.14 m/s; reference 26.81 m/s.
  • Physics: coupled droplets; provisional wall film.

What supports a steady comparison?

Mass imbalance and continuing liquid accumulation made early outlet predictions difficult to interpret. We assess three signals together:

  1. Mixture and phase mass balances.
  2. Liquid-inventory drift over a stated window.
  3. Residual level, oscillations and solver warnings.

Δṁ = ṁin − ṁout − ṁremoved

Positive removal is counted once. Steady iterations are not physical time: kg/iteration cannot be used to close a kg/s balance.

The split inlet retains 37% more liquid, yet both send over 99% of liquid feed to steam. Retention changes; an effective removal path remains missing. [2]

Droplet transport depends on diameter

F3 · 26.81 m/s · N11,000. Seven assumed fine-mist bins with carrier feedback; 5% of liquid feed allocated to droplets. Feed-weighted outcomes. [4]

The 14–35 µm bins are almost entirely incomplete; larger droplets show trapping. Unresolved tracks cannot be assigned to capture or escape.

Scientific sources and figure limits

  • Numerical comparison: Phase 8 F2 results, matched SIMPLE and Coupled/Global Time Step branches at N5,000. Same mesh and feeds; independent Hybrid initialisation; several numerical settings change. The comparison uses terminal absolute gaps and residuals at N5,000, with inventory slopes fitted over N4,500–5,000. The N10,000 gap is a final-500 mean; its inventory slope uses the same window. Linked native Phase 8 analysis bundles are absent from this checkout; the poster uses Project's reported values, not invented line histories.
  • Inventory history: Native R0 report histories, v2-total-liquid-mass. All 2,007 samples are plotted without smoothing. Windows are 3,580–4,586, 4,586–5,586 and 5,086–5,586. These are successive continuations, not a controlled single-setting experiment. R0 has full loading, smooth walls, a phase-2-only sink and EWF off; it is a separate configuration from the absorber-free Phase 8 cases.
  • R0 budgets: source-inclusive endpoint errors are recomputed from aligned phase inlet/outlet reports, native mixture outlet and applied sink, counted once. Liquid and mixture errors use their own feed denominators. An inventory derivative in kg/iteration cannot cancel a kg/s flux. The native mixture report is used rather than silently substituting a sum of phase reports.
  • Spatial view: original Fluent 2025 R2 F2 PNG, 26.81 m/s, N10,000, centre plane z=0, liquid volume fraction 0–1. SVG viewports isolate the field and native colour strip with larger vector labels; scientific pixels and colour mapping are unchanged. The offset inlet is outside this plane.
  • Inlet comparison: matched Coupled F1/F2 reference-speed results. Inventories are endpoint values; outlet/feed and area-weighted steam-face-to-outlet pressure differences are final-500 means. This is a boundary-representation comparison, not a physical geometry change or a total-pressure-loss measurement.
  • Droplet figure: F3 N11,000, 5% reference pilot. Reported injection-weighted escaped/trapped/incomplete shares are 1.88%/19.13%/78.98%, not trajectory-count fractions. Rounded values sum to 99.99%; bar widths are not renormalised. Total liquid feed is unchanged when 5.846936325 kg/s is allocated from Eulerian liquid to DPM. Incomplete feed cannot be assigned to carryover or capture.
  • Wall-film comparison: matched F3/F4 5% reference pilots at N11,000. Outlet and bulk inventory values are terminal; the 80.761 kg/s gap is a final-500 Eulerian mean, not a demonstrated complete-system leak. Film mass and maximum thickness are endpoint values. The film package is provisional, the lower boundary stays closed, and the combined transfer/fate ledger is incomplete.
