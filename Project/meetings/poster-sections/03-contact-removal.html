Give arriving liquid a lower exit

The contact-removal model represents liquid entering the omitted brine region.

Bulk liquid is removed locally in the collector. Film leaves through its lower edge, and droplets leave through collector boundaries. The corrected trial reports only 0.656 g of collector bulk liquid at N14686.

Newer corrected contact model · local liquid-only removal · native film discharge · separate droplet exits

Local contact removal replaces an inlet-throughput removal budget.

Current limit. This is an ideal lower-exit model, not a resolved brine pool. Removal varies, film storage still develops, and whole-separator conservation and steady behaviour remain unqualified.
