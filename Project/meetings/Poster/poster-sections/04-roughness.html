Roughness changes outlet flow and retained liquid

A lower steam-outlet liquid rate must be read beside inventory.

Small roughness settings increase modeled liquid outlet flow. Larger settings reduce it, while bulk liquid also depletes. R5 gives 20.6% lower tail outlet magnitude than R0, with about 161.7 kg less liquid over the run.

R0–R11 · historical roughness-only family · EWF off · earlier throughput-controlled absorber · common N5586–8586 child windows

The roughness response is not a simple “rougher means better separation” trend.

Current limit. Inventory depletion and a non-closing source-inclusive ledger limit interpretation. These historical cases do not use the newer contact-removal model.
