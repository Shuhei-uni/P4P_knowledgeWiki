# Citations and source use

## Use evidence for the actual proposition

Keep project results, external literature, assumptions, and the author's interpretation distinguishable. Use the actual study records for its findings. Use external sources for claims about prior work, established methods, mechanisms, and context where appropriate.

For a new or checked citation, inspect the relevant source passage, figure, table, or method. Confirm that it supports the exact proposition, including its scope and conditions. A matching title, search snippet, abstract-only view, or another paper's bibliography may help discovery but cannot verify details it does not contain.

Prefer original research or official technical documentation for specific technical claims. Use reviews to describe a body of work and follow their references when the underlying study matters. Verify software behaviour against the relevant version of official documentation rather than a writing guide.

## Make attribution precise

Place a citation close to the supported clause. Split a sentence when one citation would otherwise appear to support several different claims. Preserve meaningful qualifications from the source. Distinguish the source author's interpretation from this report's inference.

Paraphrase from understanding and cite the idea. Use brief, accurate quotations only when the original wording matters. Do not copy a source's distinctive structure and substitute a few words. Do not add irrelevant citations or citations to the tools used to write the report.

## Handle missing and conflicting evidence

Use a visible, specific marker such as [SOURCE NEEDED: pressure-loss comparison] during drafting when necessary. Do not invent authors, dates, titles, identifiers, page numbers, or results. If only an abstract is available, restrict claims to what it establishes and disclose the access limit when material.

Where records conflict, identify the conflict and its effect. Do not silently choose the value that improves the narrative. Check that all in-text citations have matching references and vice versa, subject to the required citation style. Preserve existing citation keys and cross-references unless asked to change them.

For an unfamiliar or current venue requirement, check its official instructions. Do not impose APA, IEEE, or another style by default when an existing report already establishes one.

These are original evidence-handling instructions. The source research is documented in [Sources](sources.md); writing-guidance sources do not supply evidence for the user's scientific findings.
