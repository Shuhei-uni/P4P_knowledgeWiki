# Grammar and sentences

## Preserve meaning while correcting language

Check subject-verb agreement, articles, singular and plural forms, prepositions, pronoun references, modifiers, and sentence boundaries. Keep comparable items grammatically parallel. Preserve values, units, conditions, negation, uncertainty, and causal meaning.

For a grammar-only request, correct the English within the requested scope. Flag a material technical ambiguity separately rather than silently choosing an interpretation or reorganising the argument.

## Choose tense by purpose

| Purpose | Usual starting point | Invented example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Completed procedure or observation | Past | We calculated the pressure drop. |
| Current model definition or relationship | Present | The model assumes a constant inlet temperature. |
| What a displayed figure contains | Present | Figure 4 shows the comparison. |
| One past event preceding another | Past perfect, when useful | The field had stabilised before sampling began. |
| Work proposed for later | Explicit proposal or future | A finer mesh will be tested in future work. |

Follow the actual timeline and discipline's conventions. A change of tense can be correct when the sentence changes purpose. Do not force an entire section into one tense.

## Check common scientific sentence problems

- Make the subject after an introductory action phrase capable of performing that action.
- Compare equivalent quantities: one case's pressure with another case's pressure.
- Place "only", "approximately", "respectively", and other modifiers where their scope is clear.
- Separate overloaded main points; retain longer sentences when their conditions and relationships remain easy to follow.
- Use technical count and mass nouns consistently. Avoid forms such as "an evidence" or "many equipment".
- Use punctuation to reveal the intended relationship. Do not repair an unclear relationship by changing punctuation alone.

Invented corrections: "The simulations was completed" becomes "The simulations were completed". "Using a finer mesh, the pressure drop was calculated" can become "We calculated the pressure drop using a finer mesh" when first person fits the document.

Basis: [MIT, Editing for grammar and style](https://web.mit.edu/21.guide/editing.htm), [MIT, Sequence of tenses](https://web.mit.edu/21.guide/sequence.htm), and [MIT, Common ESL writing problems](https://web.mit.edu/21.guide/esl-link.htm).
