# Discussion, limitations, and future work

## Explain what the results mean

Open with the answer supported by the main findings. Develop how the work addresses the research question, how it relates to relevant prior studies, and what remains unresolved. Interpret confusing or contradictory results instead of repeating the results section in a different order.

For each important interpretation, distinguish the observed result, the proposed explanation, the evidence supporting that explanation, and credible alternatives. Compare like conditions when relating the work to the literature. Agreement with one feature does not establish agreement with every relevant feature.

## Write limitations through their consequences

Describe the limitation, explain which inference it affects, and state what can still be concluded. Avoid a generic list of weaknesses with no connection to the argument. A limitation that changes a headline claim belongs beside that claim as well as in any dedicated limitations section.

Use this working table when it helps:

| Limitation | Consequence for interpretation | Supported conclusion | Useful next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Specific missing evidence, assumption, or scope boundary | Which claim becomes narrower or uncertain? | What remains defensible? | What would resolve the uncertainty? |

Do not assert that a limitation is minor without evidence. Avoid statements that all limitations can be solved simply by collecting more data or adding more model complexity.

## Make future work answer a remaining question

Connect each proposed extension to an observed limit or unresolved mechanism. Explain what it would test and how the answer could change interpretation or a design choice. Distinguish a proposed experiment from one already performed.

Illustrative only: "The wall inventory continued to change during the sampled interval. The present comparison therefore describes the developing state. A longer observation interval would help assess whether the ranking persists after the inventory stabilises."

If the work is inconclusive, explain what was learned about the method, constraints, or next discriminating test. Do not manufacture a positive conclusion to make the discussion sound complete.

Basis: [MIT Communication Lab, Discussion](https://mitcommlab.mit.edu/be/commkit/journal-article-discussion/). The limitation table and example are original applications.
