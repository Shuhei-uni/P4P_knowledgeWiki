# Entity: Pressure-Based Solver + SIMPLE + PRESTO

## Definition
Numerical stack often used for low-speed incompressible swirling flows in Fluent:
- pressure-based solver
- SIMPLE pressure-velocity coupling
- PRESTO pressure interpolation

## Usage in Wiki
- Reported for geothermal separator simulations in `purnanto-2013` ([purnanto-2013], p.6).

## Why It Matters
This stack can materially shift swirl-core pressure and separation trends. Reproductions must capture it explicitly.
